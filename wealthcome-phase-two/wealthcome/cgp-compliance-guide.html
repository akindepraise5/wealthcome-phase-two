Client file compliance in five checkpoints

A short checklist to prepare file reviews and keep next steps clear.

Adapt this guide to your status, activities and internal procedures. It offers general prompts, not legal advice.

  1. Refresh client information

    Identify missing or expiring documents and note when the file was last reviewed.

  2. Review profile and objectives

    Check that the client’s objectives, time horizon and relevant advice information are current.

  3. Check file documents

    Flag mandates, pre-contractual information and consents that need attention.

  4. Keep an advice record

    Link discussions, shared documents and agreed decisions to the next steps.

  5. Assign each deadline

    For every action, record an owner, a target date and the evidence to retain.

Before the next meeting: what needs checking, by whom and by when?
